Before you start

The questions that matter.

Do I need to know how to code?
No. You compose your store from ready-made blocks with live preview, and change anything — banner, products, pages — yourself. If you can use Instagram, you can run a Niblr store.
Can I really launch in ten minutes?
Yes, if you have your products and photos ready — look, catalog, first order. Most people spend longer choosing what to name the store than building it. Take your time if you want to; the point is that nothing technical is standing between you and being open.
Where does my money go?
Straight into your account. You connect your payment gateway with your own keys, and customers pay you directly — Niblr is never in the payment flow and never holds your money. Your keys are encrypted at rest and used only to talk to your processor.
So Niblr doesn't reduce my card processing fees?
Correct, and we won't pretend otherwise. Your gateway sets that rate and bills you directly. What changes on Niblr is everything layered on top: no platform cut of your sales, and no extra fee for choosing your own gateway — the fee Shopify charges in markets like the UAE and India.
Can I use my own web address?
Yes. Launch on a free subdomain today and attach your own domain whenever you're ready — no migration, no downtime.
Can I move away later?
Anytime. Your catalog, orders and customers export in standard formats on every plan, and the code is MIT-licensed so you can self-host the same store yourself. Leaving being easy is the reason staying feels safe.
What happens to my store if Niblr shuts down?
It keeps running. The code is MIT-licensed, your data exports in standard formats, and the hosted version is the same codebase you can deploy yourself. This is the entire reason the architecture looks the way it does.
